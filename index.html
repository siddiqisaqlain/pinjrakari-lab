From the workshop to the screen

Pinjrakari

Kashmiri lattice screens, built from small pieces of wood, usually walnut or deodar, fitted together without nails. Try the strapwork view to read a pattern as wooden strips.

Khatamband

Ceilings made of small interlocking wooden pieces that form geometric stars. The 8-point and 12-point presets work in the same family of geometry.

The method

Hankin's method puts rays on the midpoint of each tiling edge and trims them where they meet. The contact angle decides how open or tight the star becomes.
